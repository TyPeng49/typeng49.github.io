Research in progress · Sep. 2025–Present

Two-sided Matching with Exogenous Uncertainty: Marriage Market

Joint with Zhuzhu Zhou

A one-to-one two-sided matching model studying how uncertainty over potential partners affects matching incentives and equilibrium marriage outcomes. The project develops baseline and ideal-partner mechanisms and characterizes their blocking and stability conditions.

02

Working paper · Sep. 2025–Jun. 2026

A Dual-Benchmark Measure of Inequality of Opportunity

Joint with Zhuzhu Zhou · Presented at PHBS Thinktank Conference 2026

A dual-benchmark framework integrating ex-ante and ex-post principles within a unified Rawlsian approach. Using CHIP data, instrumental variables, and Shapley decomposition, the paper documents cohort trends and regional heterogeneity in inequality of opportunity.

InequalityApplied MicroeconomicsCausal Inference

National Undergraduate Innovation Program · Dec. 2024–Jun. 2026

Industrial Development & Financing Dynamics in the Haixi Economic Zone

Core team member

A firm-level panel study using CSMAR data from 2003–2023 and the 2011 Haixi Development Plan as a quasi-natural experiment. The analysis finds reduced financing constraints and positive spatial spillovers beyond central cities.
